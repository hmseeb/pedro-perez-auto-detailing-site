About Us

Passion for Perfection,
One Car at a Time

Pedro Perez Auto Detailing was founded on a simple belief: every vehicle deserves to look its absolute best. With over a decade of hands-on experience, Pedro and his team bring unmatched attention to detail, premium products, and genuine care to every job — no matter the make, model, or condition.

We use only professional-grade, eco-conscious products that are tough on dirt and grime but safe for your vehicle's surfaces, clear coat, and the environment. Whether you drive a daily commuter or a prized weekend cruiser, we treat it with the same respect and dedication.

  • Certified and experienced detailing technicians
  • Professional-grade, eco-friendly products
  • Mobile detailing available — we come to you
  • 100% satisfaction guarantee on every service
